**There is no federal clean vehicle purchase credit for an EV acquired in 2026.** The New Clean Vehicle Credit (§30D), Previously-Owned Clean Vehicle Credit (§25E), and Qualified Commercial Clean Vehicle Credit (§45W) are unavailable for vehicles acquired after **September 30, 2025**, according to the [IRS clean vehicle tax credit guidance](https://www.irs.gov/clean-vehicle-tax-credits).

An eligible vehicle acquired by the deadline may still qualify even if it was placed in service later. The IRS says a binding written contract and a payment made by September 30, 2025 can establish acquisition. Taking delivery alone is not the test. Keep the contract, payment record, seller report, and other documentation, and check the [IRS instructions for Form 8936](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8936) before filing.

## What this means for a 2026 buyer

| Situation | Federal clean vehicle purchase credit |
| --- | --- |
| You acquire a new or used EV in 2026 | **None** under §30D or §25E |
| A business or lessor acquires a qualifying EV in 2026 | **None** under §45W |
| You acquired an eligible EV by September 30, 2025 and take delivery later | May still qualify, subject to the earlier rules and documentation |

A dealer discount or a manufacturer's lease offer in 2026 may still reduce your price. It is a commercial promotion, **not** a newly available federal clean vehicle credit. Ask the seller for the offer's terms and expiration date.

## Are there EV incentives left?

State, local, and utility programs are separate from the expired federal vehicle credits. They change by location, funding, income, vehicle, and application date. Check the program administrator's current terms before including any rebate in your budget. Our [state incentive directory](/ev-incentives) is a starting point for finding programs, not an eligibility determination.

The separate federal home charging equipment credit had its own deadline: the IRS says qualifying property had to be placed in service **before July 1, 2026**. See the [IRS Alternative Fuel Vehicle Refueling Property Credit guidance](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/alternative-fuel-vehicle-refueling-property-credit) for historical claims and requirements. A new home charger installed after that date does not qualify for that federal credit.

## How to compare EV costs without an expired credit

1. Start with the current, written vehicle price and financing or lease offer.
2. Enter your actual electricity price, annual mileage, insurance quote, and expected charging mix.
3. Add a state or utility rebate only after checking current eligibility and funding with its administrator.
4. Compare the total cost over the period you plan to own or lease the vehicle.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I claim $7,500 for a new EV bought in 2026?

No. The federal §30D credit is unavailable for a vehicle acquired after September 30, 2025. An eligible vehicle acquired by that deadline may still qualify if placed in service later; see the IRS guidance linked above.

### Can I get $4,000 for a used EV bought in 2026?

No. The federal §25E previously-owned clean vehicle credit has the same acquisition deadline.

### Does leasing restore the federal credit in 2026?

No. The §45W commercial clean vehicle credit is also unavailable for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025. A lease promotion may offer a discount for another reason.

### Can I claim a state rebate?

Possibly. State and utility incentives have their own rules. Confirm the program is open and funded, then check your vehicle and household eligibility directly with the administrator.
